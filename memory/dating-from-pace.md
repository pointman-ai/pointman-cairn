---
name: dating-from-pace
description: How to set start and target dates people can believe
metadata:
  type: reference
---

- Measure the team's real pace from closed items (created, started, closed) and the commit log, not from estimates.
- **Starting assumptions until you've measured:**
  - a well-scoped agent task takes 1–4 hours once it's unblocked;
  - an epic takes half a day to a few days;
  - reviews land the next working day;
  - steps that wait on a person are usually the bottleneck.
- Date a task's start after the work it needs, never before.
- When a date passes, move it and say why in the item. Don't leave it red.

**Why:** dates nobody believes get ignored, and then the board stops being read.
**How to apply:** recalibrate the assumptions after the first week's closed items. Related: [[board-shape]].
